# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude Code in this repository.

## What this is

A small WebXR garden outside one of the hallway's doors (three.js 0.186, plain ES modules, no
bundler or build step): a lawn, a stream under a foot bridge into a pond, a trail to a bench, and a
book on the bench to read, with vrlbry's book controls (AriaMoKr/vrlbry, a checkout at `../vrlbry`:
its toolbar, contents, text sizes, themes, one page or two, distance and size, links and Back, and
text view). It is a place behind a door of the Hallway (https://ariamokr.github.io/hallway/,
AriaMoKr/hallway, a checkout at `../hallway`), whose conventions and controls it follows; much of
its code is the hallway's (said so at the top of each such file). `public/` is the whole site.
`README.md` covers using it, what it does with the hallway's door link, the hallway's door entry for
here (its fourth door, since the hallway's `9dd2d5a`), and the pictures either side of that door.
The user asked for the hallway to be left alone, so change `../hallway` only when asked.

Meant to publish at https://ariamokr.github.io/outside/ from `main` of AriaMoKr/outside by
`.github/workflows/pages.yml` (same origin as the hallway, so the VR hand-over works).

## Commands

```bash
npm install                  # postinstall: common's tools/vendor.mjs copies what vendor.json lists (three, common, layersharedsession, Trystero, and IWER) into public/vendor/ (gitignored)
npm start                    # tools/serve.mjs (common's, on port 8090): public/ on http://localhost:8090/ (IPv6 only on Windows; for a Quest: npm start -- --host 127.0.0.1)
npm test                     # node --test "test/*.test.js"
node tools/preview.mjs       # public/hallway-preview.jpg, the garden for the hallway's doorway (headless Chrome)
node tools/hallway-view.mjs  # public/views/, the hall seen out of its door to here, from a copy of ../hallway (the door added if it has none)
```

Node 22 or newer (`engines`; common's `tools/chrome.mjs` needs its built-in WebSocket); CI runs Node 24 (common's Pages workflow). It runs under MSYS2 UCRT64 here (`MSYSTEM=UCRT64 /c/msys64/usr/bin/bash -lc 'cd /c/git/my/claude/outside && …'`).

## Layout

- `garden.js`: pure (no three, tested in Node). Where everything is (`GARDEN`, `DOOR`, `HOUSE`, `PANEL`, `BENCH`, `SEAT`, `BOOK`, `BRIDGE`, `TRAIL`, `TREES`), the stream (`streamAt`, the widths, `waterLevel`), the ground (`terrain`, sampled once on `GRID` by `layoutGarden`; `groundAt` reads the grid, and the deck on the bridge), what is on top for a visitor's ring (`surfaceAt`: the ground, or the water over its very edge), walking (`clampWalk`: hedges, wall, house but the doorway, trunks, bench, the bridge's rails, the water; `standable`), the way (`route`: straight when clear, else A* on the grid, corners cut), `doorCrossed`, `doorSpawn`, `castRay` (what a pointer meets: door, panel, wall, book, bench, tree, hedge, wall, water, ground; the ground by marching the grid). Coordinates: metres, the house's wall at z = 0, the garden along -z, the door at x = 0, yaw 0 faces -z.
- The door contract is AriaMoKr/common's `door.js` (`common/door.js` in the import map and in Node): the hallway's door link (`readVisit`), the way back (`wayBack(search, OUTSIDE_ID, { origin })`, `keptWay`, `leaveUrl`), the address cleaned (`withoutVisit`), where the hallway's view of its door to here is (`hallwayViews(way, OUTSIDE_ID)`, `viewParams`). `way.js` keeps what is this garden's: `OUTSIDE_ID`, `DEFAULT_GLOW`, and `HALLWAY_DOOR`, the hallway's door entry for here (kept in step with README's).
- `pages.js`: pure. The book set in pages (`paginate`, with a `measure(text, font)` function and a text size, `scale`: vrlbry's `FONT_STEPS`, `typeAt`; the contents and the sources run over onto more pages when they must), spreads (`spreadPages`, `spreadOf`, `sideOf`), and every line's place in the book, `at` = [section, block, word] (`SECTION`: the title page, the contents, each piece, the sources; the paragraph, stanza, entry or note, -1 for a heading; the words of the block before it), a page's `at` its first line's: `pageAt` finds a place's page at any size, which is how the reader stays at the same words. The contents page's entries are links (`page.links`, `linkAt`). The open pages' words for a screen reader (`readingText`: the lines put back into headings, paragraphs, stanzas and the contents' list, from the `start`, `carried`, `spoken` and page `kind` marks `paginate` leaves; `spreadLabel`), and each part as blocks for the text view (`sectionBlocks`, numbered as `at` numbers them). `book.onShow` says the pages are open (on opening, as a page turns, at another size); `overlay.showBookText` puts their words in a visually hidden, politely live region named for the book (not while the text view shows them). `reading.js`: the book's text (public domain, sources named; check a change against them).
- `book.js`: the book closed on the bench and open in front of the reader (pages painted when first needed in the reader's theme, the leaf turning over, a page at a time sliding across with only its half of the book shown once still), read with vrlbry's controls: the toolbar under the pages and the contents beside or over them (canvas panels, `canvas-panel.js`: vrlbry's `ui/panel.js` Panel, cut down), `changeFont` (set again, at the same words; steps one after another keep to the words the first started from), `setTheme`/`cycleTheme`, `togglePages`/`setSingle`, `adjustDistance`/`adjustScale`, `grab`/`release` (a VR grip), `toFront`, the contents page's links with a trail (`followLink`, `goBack`, `backTo`, `back`), `jumpToPlace` (the text view's), and `pick`/`setHover`/`press` for every pointer. On a screen `_fit` places it so the pages and the toolbar fill the view above a strip at its foot (corners projected: the pages' curve and the toolbar come nearer than the book's middle); in VR it is held where it was last (`settings.readDistance`). `reader.js` (pure): vrlbry's themes (`THEMES`; the text view's CSS has the same colours, tested), `singlePage`/`pageViewSetting`, `fontStep`, `READ`'s limits, and `readerSettings`, the reader's choices as stored (`outside.book` in localStorage, main.js; this site's own). `text-view.js`: vrlbry's text view (`ui/text-view.js`), here the book's parts (the title page with the contents, a piece, the sources: `renderPart`, pure and tested with vrlbry's fake DOM), keeping its place with the 3D book to the word (`wordStarts`, `firstWordBelow`, `_topBlock`, `_sync`; `_scrollTo` measures the toolbar itself, since its observer hasn't run when the view has just been shown, and opening checks the place again on the next frame). The browser's Back along the links, then putting the book down, is common's `back-history.js` (an entry when it opens and per link, taken back out when it is put down otherwise), with `prefix: 'outside'`: its fields keep the names this site's own copy gave them (`outsideBook`…), so entries already in visitors' tabs are still the book's.
- `scene.js` builds everything as a few merged meshes with light baked into vertex colours (`bake.js`: a low sun traced through the occluders it is given, crowns letting some through by the leaf crossed; sky light; point lights). It calls `plants.js` (trees, hedges, grass/flowers/reeds, the willow's strands), `features.js` (bridge, bench, dry-stone wall, rocks, lily pads, water-gates), `house.js` (the wall, windows, roof, doorcase, the sign and plaque, the panel, the portal), `water.js` (the stream and pond, one ribbon, its shader), and common's `sky.js` (`skyTexture`: the sky painted once, equirectangular; `skyDome` looks it up by direction; the water reflects it through `SKY_UV`, and `bake.js` lights by its `SUN_DIR`). The garden's sky is common's: every window behind every door shows it, so a change to it is made in common (its `src/sky.js`, then a new tag here), never in a copy here. `builder.js` is the hallway's Builder with cuboids, cylinders, lumpy spheres and triangles added. Grass and strands sway in a vertex shader (`sway` attribute).
- The doorway's shader is common's `portal.js` (`portalMaterial`, `placePortal`, and `setView` for a picture hung by views.json's parameters), which `house.js` uses. `textures.js`: canvas textures, seeded.
- `controls.js`: the hallway's controls, standing on the ground, routes round the water, sitting (`sit`, `stand`, `seated`), back to the start (`resetTo`), and while the book is open vrlbry's book controls: presses on it (`book.pick` first, `onBookPress`), the page keys (`onPage`), and the rest as `onBookAction(action, value)`: its keys (`BOOK_KEYS`, + −, ↑ ↓ in the contents), the wheel and a two-finger pinch (`distance`), a quick touch swipe (a page, the view left as it was), a gamepad's buttons (`PAD_BOOK`, the triggers), and in VR the right stick (pages, distance), the left stick (`scale`; walking waits until it is down), a thumbstick pressed (`front`), B or Y (`back`) and a grip (`onGrab`, instead of a wave). Home is `onHome` anywhere. A gamepad is read by common's `gamepad.js` (`firstPad`, `readPad`, `held`), and the fade is common's `fade.js` (`Fade`), as in the hallway. The VR movement choice is common's `nav.js` (`hallway.nav` in localStorage, shared with the hallway).
- Accessibility settings are common's `access.js` (`hallway.access`, shared with every site on the origin; `main.js` `applyAccess`, `setAccess`, re-read on a `storage` event and on `pageshow`): with less motion (`reduce`) `controls.reduceMotion` makes every walk a jump (`_jump`: a click, the bridge, the bench; the door is gone through at once), the fades blink (`blink`), `book.reduceMotion` cuts the leaf's turn and the narrow screen's slide, `together.reduceMotion` skips the wave's bob, the `reduce-motion` class on `<html>` stills the overlay, and the frame loop's `time` (the water, the grass's sway, the doorway's veil) stops. A `Vignette` sits beside the `Fade`, fed `controls.moveSpeed` and `turnRate` (the sticks' smooth movement and turning; 0 for snaps and teleports). `controls.setEyeLift(eyeLift(access))` raises the rig in VR only; on the bench its own lift (from the headset's height) holds, so seated mode and the bench agree.
- `preview.js`: the picture for the hallway's doorway, as the hallway hangs it.
- `panel.js`, `overlay.js`: the hallway's, the panel on the house's wall, the overlay's card (the page's `<nav>`) with the way back instead of doors, and the garden's 3D choices as real controls for a keyboard and a screen reader (Sit on the bench / Get up, Read the book from anywhere, Start / Leave shared session, Settings: the panel's Motion, Move and Turn as radio buttons and its Vignette and Seated as checkboxes, kept in step with it by `showPanel`; for the book, Open it as text, Bring the book in front of me, Reset view), the session as page `outside`. While the book is open the card goes out of its way as vrlbry's does (`#overlay.reading`), back on `:focus-within`, and Text view is a pill by the buttons at the foot. The hint keeps clear of those buttons by measuring them (`_placeHint`, whenever they or the window change): in the middle, else beside them, wrapping, else above them. The view (the canvas, `role="img"` and named), its hint and buttons, and the text view are the page's `<main>`; `controls.js` leaves a focused form control its keys, a focused button its Space and Enter, and the text view (`data-own-keys`) all of them. The shared session is layersharedsession's session controller (`together.js`, `createTogether`; outside's `together.js` makes it, `gardenTogether`: page `outside`, `avatars: { parent: scene, ground: surfaceAt }` so the visitors' rings sit on the terrain) and words its notices the garden's way (`noticeText`: join, arrive, leave, wave). It keeps `history.state` when it puts the room in the address, so the book's Back entries survive; `pause()` resolves once left, and the door waits for it (half a second at most) after its fade.
- `main.js`: renderer, the visit and the way back, arrival, going back in (`go`: fade, then `location.assign`), the bench and the book (`openBook`, `closeBook`, `back`, the book's actions, the reader's choices in `outside.book`, `BackHistory` on `popstate`, the `TextView`, `resetView`), VR (`sessiongranted`, noted by the inline script in index.html), the panel, the session, the frame loop.

## Conventions

- Client URLs stay relative (tested). Quest 3 is the performance target: few draw calls (about 15; the open book adds three: its two halves, each its board and the pages under it merged, and the toolbar), light baked, nothing allocated per frame in controls/castRay/the book's pick and hover/the frame loop (a panel is redrawn only when what it shows changes). Build time is ~350 ms; keep it well inside the ~5 s a VR grant lasts.
- Scripted testing: `window.__outside` (renderer, scene, camera, rig, garden, layout, controls, overlay, fade, vignette, book, textView, backHistory, way, visit, `go`, `enterVR`, `openBook`, `closeBook`, `resetView`, `setAccess(change)`, `access` (the settings and `reduce`), `preview()`, `tick(dt, n)`, `xrDevice`, together). With no XR frames (the app's window not open), VR logic can still be driven while presenting: give `controls.hands[i].source` stand-ins `{ handedness, targetRayMode: 'tracked-pointer', gamepad: { axes, buttons } }` (seven buttons: 3 the thumbstick, 5 B or Y) before each single `tick` (IWER's own sources replace them when it connects them), and set `camera.position.y` (the headset's height) before each, since a render puts the camera back to the XR pose (at the floor, with no frames: open the book after setting it). An XR controller's `matrixAutoUpdate` is off: move or aim one by its `matrix`. A hidden browser pane gets no animation frames, and so no scroll events: drive it with `tick()`, call `textView._rest()` after scrolling the text view, and render a frame before a screenshot (otherwise it shows a stale frame). XR frames under `?xr=emulate` need the pane's tab in front (`tabs_select`); aim with `xrDevice.controllers.right.position/quaternion` (rig-local), `updateButtonValue('trigger', 1)`, `updateAxes('thumbstick', x, y)`. A gamepad: replace `navigator.getGamepads` and dispatch `gamepadconnected`, as in the hallway. A touch: `PointerEvent`s with `pointerType: 'touch'` on the canvas (the pane's own clicks are a mouse's).
- `.claude/launch.json`: `outside` (8090), `outside-quest` (127.0.0.1:8091), and `hallway` (../hallway on 8088) for trying the door both ways.
- Headless Chrome is common's `tools/chrome.mjs` (`launchChrome`, which preview.mjs and hallway-view.mjs use): it writes the fresh profile's `Local State` first so Windows sees no failed logon. Start Chrome for a tool through it, never by hand on a new profile.
- Shared packages, pinned by tag in package.json: AriaMoKr/common (`v0.5.0`: the door contract, `hallway.nav`, the doorway's portal, the fade, the gamepad, the sky, the accessibility settings with the vignette, the book's Back (`back-history.js`), and the tools: `serve.mjs`, `vendor.mjs` with this site's `vendor.json`, `chrome.mjs`, and the Pages workflow `.github/workflows/pages.yml` calls) and AriaMoKr/layersharedsession (`v0.3.0`: the session, the avatars and the session controller). Change them there, then move the tag here and `npm install`; tests that belong to them live there.
- Code still copied from other repos, until it moves into a shared one: `public/js/canvas-panel.js` is vrlbry's `ui/panel.js` Panel cut to buttons, text, sliders and a list without scroll bars (its `set` redraws only on a change, and nothing is allocated over it); `public/js/text-view.js` follows vrlbry's `ui/text-view.js` (its toolbar and markup, its place kept with the 3D book) for this book's parts; `test/helpers/fake-dom.js` is vrlbry's. A fix to one of them belongs in its original too, and one made there belongs here.
- Link previews: the `og:` and `twitter:` tags in `index.html` show `hallway-preview.jpg`, the picture the hallway's door uses (a test keeps them matching `HALLWAY_DOOR` and the picture's size), so render it again with `tools/preview.mjs` when the garden changes.
